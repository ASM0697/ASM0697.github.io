When I was in the college, I always needed to track my GPA after each semester and plan for the next one. I liked to run simulations—assuming I earned certain grades in specific courses—and see how my cumulative GPA (cGPA) would turn out. This approach helped me a lot to stay on track and reduce academic stress, especially during semesters with heavy scientific content.

I created this simple Excel file to make the process practical and straightforward. It follows a grading system where A+ = 4 and A = 4.

✅ You can use this file on your phone or laptop—whether your degree program is 4 or 5 years—to calculate:

1. The GPA for each semester along with its credit hours.
2. The cGPA for each academic year separately, along with its total credit hours.
3. The overall cGPA across all academic years, along with total credit hours.
4. Most importantly, you can run virtual simulations for future courses and see how achieving certain grades would affect your cGPA and where it would end up.

🔗 Online sheet with the option to download for offline use: http://bit.ly/4ln3Ssy
